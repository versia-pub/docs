The Reactions Extension allows users to express their reactions ("react") to posts with emojis.

## Delivery and Access

A sender **must** [deliver](/federation#delivery) Reactions to the author of the [Note](/entities/note) and, if the Note is public, to the followers of the reacting user.

Any instance that [may fetch](/federation#access) the Note, and the instance of the Note's author, may fetch a Reaction.

Reactions can be displayed to clients depending on the privacy settings of the associated Note.

## Note Collections

The Reactions extension adds the following collections to the [Note](/entities/note) entity:

- `pub.versia:reactions/Reactions`: [Reference Collection](/structures/collection#reference-collection) of all the reactions to the note.

## Interaction Types

<Note>
    This section only applies to implementors of the [Interaction Controls Extension](/extensions/interaction-controls).
</Note>

This extension registers the following interaction types:
- `pub.versia:reactions#React`, for adding a Reaction to a Note.
