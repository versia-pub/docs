The Reactions Extension allows users to express their reactions ("react") to notes with emojis.

## Definition

<Entity name="Reaction" type="pub.versia:reactions/Reaction">

    </Field>
    <Field name="author" type="Reference" required>
        [Reference](/entities#reference) to the [User](/entities/user) that is reacting.

    <Field name="target" type="Reference" required>
        [Reference](/entities#reference) to the [Note](/entities/note) attached to the reaction.
    </Field>
    <Field name="content" type="string" required>
        Emoji content of reaction. Can also be arbitrary text, or the `name` of a [Custom Emoji](/extensions/custom-emojis), such as `:happy_face:`, with a matching entry in this Reaction's own `pub.versia:custom_emojis` extension. **MUST** be at least 1 character long.

        Receivers **MAY** drop a Reaction they cannot display.
    </Field>

## Behavior

To undo a Reaction, a [Delete](/entities/delete) entity **MUST** be used. The `deleted` property of the Delete entity **MUST** be a [Reference](/entities#reference) to the Reaction to be removed.

## Delivery and Access

A sender **MUST** [deliver](/federation#delivery) Reactions to the author of the [Note](/entities/note) and, if the Note is public, to the followers of the reacting user.

Any instance that [may fetch](/federation#access) the Note, and the instance of the Note's author, may fetch a Reaction.

Reactions can be displayed to clients depending on the privacy settings of the associated Note.

## Collections

The Reactions extension adds the following collections to the [Note](/entities/note) entity:

- `pub.versia:reactions/Reactions`: [Reference Collection](/structures/collection#reference-collection) of all the reactions to the note.

## Interaction Types

This extension registers the following [interaction types](/extensions/interaction-controls#interaction-types):
- `pub.versia:reactions/Reaction`, for adding a Reaction to a Note.
