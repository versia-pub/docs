The Share Extension lets users share notes they like with others. This is the same as Twitter's "retweet" and Mastodon's "boost".

## Behaviour

A user **MUST NOT** share a [Note](/entities/note) whose `visibility` is not `public`. A receiver **MUST** ignore a Share of such a Note.

In clients, `Shares` **SHOULD** be rendered in a way that makes it clear that the shared note was originally authored by someone else than the user who shared it.

`Shares` can be undone ("unboosting") with a [Delete](/entities/delete) entity.

## Delivery and Access

A sender **MUST** [deliver](/federation#delivery) Shares to the author of the [Note](/entities/note) and to the followers of the user who shared it.

Any instance that [may fetch](/federation#access) the Note, and the instance of the Note's author, may fetch a Share.

## Collections

The Share extension adds the following collections to the [Note](/entities/note) entity:

- `pub.versia:share/Shares`: [Reference Collection](/structures/collection#reference-collection) of all the shares of the note.

## Interaction Types

This extension registers the following [interaction types](/extensions/interaction-controls#interaction-types):
- `pub.versia:share/Share`, for sharing a Note.
