Rejects a pending [Subscribe](/entities/subscribe), or removes a subscriber after the subscription has been accepted. How subscriptions work is described under [Subscribe](/entities/subscribe#behavior).

## Definition

`SubscribeReject` has no `author`. It **MUST** be sent by the target's instance. Receivers check this as [Subscribe describes](/entities/subscribe#checking-the-sender).

<Entity name="SubscribeReject" type="SubscribeReject" transient checker>

    <Field name="target" type="Reference" required>

    </Field>
    <Field name="target_type" type="string" required>
