A [User](/entities/user) can subscribe to another entity to receive what it shares with its subscribers. The `Subscribe` entity starts a subscription.

- **Subscriber**: The user who subscribes. If `Joe` subscribes to `Alice`, `Joe` is the subscriber.
- **Target**: The entity subscribed to. If `Joe` subscribes to `Alice`, `Alice` is the target.

Only entity types whose page says they **accept subscriptions** can be a target. In the core, that is the [User](/entities/user). An extension can make its own types accept subscriptions, as [Groups](/extensions/groups) does, and states on its page what their subscribers receive.

## Definition

`Subscribe` has no `author`. It **MUST** be sent by the subscriber's instance.

### Checking the Sender

None of the four subscription entities has an `author`. When a receiver checks [Authors and Signers](/validation#authors-and-signers), it **MUST** treat `subscriber` as the author of a `Subscribe` or an [Unsubscribe](/entities/unsubscribe), and `target` as the author of a [SubscribeAccept](/entities/subscribe-accept) or a [SubscribeReject](/entities/subscribe-reject).

`target_type` **MUST** be the type of the entity that `target` points to. A receiver that holds that entity **MUST** [reject](/validation#rejecting-entities) a subscription entity whose `target_type` does not match it.

## Behavior

`Joe`, a user on `joe.example`, wants to subscribe to `Alice`, a user on `alice.example`.

### Sending a Subscription Request

To start a subscription, `joe.example` **MUST** do the following:

1. Create a `Subscribe` entity with `Joe` as the subscriber and `Alice` as the target.
2. Send the `Subscribe` entity to `alice.example`'s inbox.
3. Treat the subscription as "processing" until a `SubscribeAccept` or `SubscribeReject` arrives, or until `Joe` sends an [Unsubscribe](/entities/unsubscribe).

A `Subscribe` whose `target_type` does not accept subscriptions **MUST** be [rejected](/validation#rejecting-entities).

### Accepting the Subscription Request

`alice.example` **MUST** answer every `Subscribe` with a [SubscribeAccept](/entities/subscribe-accept) or a [SubscribeReject](/entities/subscribe-reject), even if [`manually_approves_subscribers`](/entities/user#user-manually-approves-subscribers) is `false`. If `Joe` is already subscribed to `Alice` and sends another `Subscribe`, `alice.example` **MUST** send a `SubscribeAccept` again, in case the first was lost.

The answer can wait for `Alice` to decide. There is no time limit on a pending `Subscribe`.

To accept the subscription request, `alice.example` **MUST** do the following:

1. Create a [SubscribeAccept](/entities/subscribe-accept) entity with `Joe` as the subscriber and `Alice` as the target.
2. Send the `SubscribeAccept` entity to `joe.example`'s inbox.

It then treats `Joe` as a subscriber of `Alice`.

### Rejecting the Subscription Request

To reject the subscription request, `alice.example` **MUST** do the following:

1. Create a [SubscribeReject](/entities/subscribe-reject) entity with `Joe` as the subscriber and `Alice` as the target.
2. Send the `SubscribeReject` entity to `joe.example`'s inbox.

### Cancelling the Subscription Request

To cancel a subscription request that has not been answered, `joe.example` sends an [Unsubscribe](/entities/unsubscribe).

### Final Steps

`joe.example` **MUST** mark the subscription as "accepted" when it receives a `SubscribeAccept`, and remove it when it receives a `SubscribeReject`.

### Delivering Entities

Which entities reach a subscriber is defined in [Delivery](/federation#delivery), and by the page of each type that accepts subscriptions.

### Ending the Subscription

`Joe` ends the subscription by sending an [Unsubscribe](/entities/unsubscribe). `Alice` ends it by sending a [SubscribeReject](/entities/subscribe-reject), which removes `Joe` as a subscriber. An `Unsubscribe` is **not** used to remove a subscriber.

### Flows

Every message here is sent to the inbox of the other user's instance. A subscription request is either accepted:

<WireTrace a="joe.example" b="alice.example">
    <Message from="a" label="Subscribe">`Joe` asks to subscribe to `Alice`.</Message>
    <Message from="a" local>Marks the subscription as "processing".</Message>
    <Message from="b" request label="SubscribeAccept">`Alice` accepts.</Message>
    <Message from="b" local>Marks the subscription as "accepted".</Message>
    <Message from="a" local>Marks the subscription as "accepted".</Message>
</WireTrace>

Or rejected:

<WireTrace a="joe.example" b="alice.example">
    <Message from="a" label="Subscribe">`Joe` asks to subscribe to `Alice`.</Message>
    <Message from="a" local>Marks the subscription as "processing".</Message>
    <Message from="b" request label="SubscribeReject">`Alice` rejects.</Message>
    <Message from="b" local>Optionally, logs the rejection.</Message>
    <Message from="a" local>Removes the subscription.</Message>
</WireTrace>

Once accepted, the subscription ends when `Joe` [unsubscribes](/entities/unsubscribe):

<WireTrace a="joe.example" b="alice.example">
    <Message from="a" label="Unsubscribe">`Joe` unsubscribes from `Alice`.</Message>
    <Message from="a" local>Removes the subscription.</Message>
    <Message from="b" local>Removes the subscription.</Message>
</WireTrace>

Or when `Alice` removes `Joe` as a subscriber:

<WireTrace a="joe.example" b="alice.example">
    <Message from="b" request label="SubscribeReject">`Alice` removes `Joe` as a subscriber.</Message>
    <Message from="b" local>Removes the subscription.</Message>
    <Message from="a" local>Removes the subscription.</Message>
</WireTrace>
