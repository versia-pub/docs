Accepts a pending [Subscribe](/entities/subscribe). How subscriptions work is described under [Subscribe](/entities/subscribe#behavior).

## Definition

`SubscribeAccept` has no `author`. It **MUST** be sent by the target's instance. Receivers check this as [Subscribe describes](/entities/subscribe#checking-the-sender).

<Entity name="SubscribeAccept" type="SubscribeAccept" transient checker>
    <Field name="subscriber" type="Reference" required>
        [Reference](/entities#reference) to the [User](/entities/user) whose subscription is accepted.
    </Field>
    <Field name="target" type="Reference" required>
        [Reference](/entities#reference) to the entity subscribed to.
    </Field>
    <Field name="target_type" type="string" required>
        Type of the entity that `target` points to, such as `User`.
    </Field>
