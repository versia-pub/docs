Ends a follow relationship, or [cancels a pending follow request](/entities/follow#cancelling-the-follow-request). It is sent by the follower. How follow relationships work is described under [Follow](/entities/follow#behavior).

## Definition

<Entity name="Unfollow" type="Unfollow" transient checker>
    <Field name="author" type="Reference" required>
        [Reference](/entities#reference) to the `User` considered the 'follower', i.e. the user who is unsubscribing from the followee.
    </Field>
    <Field name="followee" type="Reference" required>
        [Reference](/entities#reference) to the `User` considered the 'followee', i.e. the user who is being unsubscribed from.
    </Field>
