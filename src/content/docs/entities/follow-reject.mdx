Rejects a pending [Follow](/entities/follow), or removes a follower after the relationship has been established. How follow relationships work is described under [Follow](/entities/follow#behaviour).

## Definition

<Entity name="FollowReject" type="FollowReject" transient>
    <Field name="author" type="Reference" required>
        [Reference](/entities#reference) to the `User` considered the 'followee', i.e. the user who is being followed.
    </Field>
    <Field name="follower" type="Reference" required>
        [Reference](/entities#reference) to the `User` considered the 'follower', i.e. the user who is trying to follow the author.
    </Field>
